<p align="center"><b>A stereo filter whose two sides can drift apart.</b></p>

Strait gives you one filter per side, both driven by the same controls, so a mono sound comes out as a matched pair. Then **SPREAD** pulls the left and right cutoffs apart, up to an octave each way, and the sound opens out across the speakers. Put a slow LFO on SPREAD and the filtering sways from side to side.

**DRIFT** lets each side wander on its own, slowly, so the stereo image never quite stands still. A Type parameter picks low pass, band pass or high pass, and Mix blends back the dry sound. Turn **RES** to the top and the filter sings by itself, a clean pair of tones that follow V/OCT.

It is built for ambient work and kept deliberately simple: three pots over three pages, and every parameter takes CV through the disting NT's own mappings.

## Installing

Strait is free. It needs disting NT [firmware 1.19 or newer](https://www.expert-sleepers.co.uk/distingNTfirmwareupdates.html). Download `strait-plugin.zip` from the latest release, unzip it at the root of the SD card (it installs `strait.o` at `/programs/plug-ins/ormermodular/strait.o`), restart the module or remount the card, then add Strait.

The manual covers every control, the three pages and the CV mappings.
